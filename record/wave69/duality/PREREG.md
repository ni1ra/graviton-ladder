## 1. Claim under test

**Theorem (to prove or refute).** Let A be the net on the Fock space over the helicity ±2 one-particle space, with A(O) generated by the Weyl operators of the linearised curvature (equivalently its electric and magnetic parts E_ij, B_ij) smeared in O. For every double cone O = D(B), B an open ball in a time slice,

  A(O)′ = A(O′), where A(O′) is generated by the A(O₁) with O₁ a double cone in the spacelike complement of O.

**Planned route (direct, no Hislop, no conformal group).** The helicity-2 analogue of the Araki / Leyland–Roberts–Testard / Camassa proof for the scalar and Maxwell fields:
1. Araki's abstract duality R(K)′ = R(K′) reduces the claim to real subspaces of the one-particle space: K(O)′ = K(O′).
2. In the variables (E, B) at time zero the one-particle space is pairs of transverse traceless symmetric tensors in Ḣ^{-3/2}(ℝ³), and [E(f), B(g)] = i c ∫ g·Cf, with C the linearised Cotton–York operator (third order, local).
3. L(U) := closed span of the vectors of E(f), B(g), with f, g test tensors supported in U. By construction L(U)′ = N(ℝ³∖U) := {vectors whose E and B are supported in ℝ³∖U}.
4. Lemma A (analysis): for a ball, every element of N(B̄) is a limit of smooth TT pairs compactly supported in B (dilate inward, then mollify).
5. Lemma B (topology): a smooth TT field compactly supported in an open U with H²_c(U) = 0 is C f with f compactly supported in U. For the ball this gives N(B̄) = L(B).
6. Then L(ext)′ = N(B̄) = L(B), so L(B)′ = L(ext), and a partition of unity on the test tensors gives L(ext) ⊂ K(O′).
7. For regions with H₁ ≠ 0, step 5 fails by H²_c(U) ⊗ (10 conformal Killing vectors), for E and for B: the 20 charges of Benedetti–Casini–Magán.

The conformal route (Lledó + Brunetti–Guido–Longo) is examined only to say what it does and does not give.

## 3. Computations and plants

### S1: symbolic identities (`scripts/s1_identities.py`, sympy, Fourier symbols)
Each is an exact polynomial identity in k:
- a. C f := curl(inc f − ½ δ tr inc f) is symmetric, traceless and divergence-free for every symmetric f.
- b. C(Kv) = 0 for K v = ∂_(i v_j) and C(ψ δ) = 0.
- c. The symbol of C is Hermitian (C formally self-adjoint).
- d. On TT tensors curl² = −Δ, and curl Δ P_TT f = c·C f for one constant c (the canonical commutator [E(f), B(g)] is local).
- e. The chain in Lemma B: the algebraic identities used between the three Poincaré-lemma steps (symmetry of T ⇔ divergence condition on A; antisymmetric part of the potential drops out of inc).
- f. The 10 conformal Killing vectors give ∂_i(T_ij ξ_j) = 0 for TT T.
- **Plant S1-P:** the same checks on a broken operator C_bad := curl(inc f) (trace correction dropped). At least one of a, b must fail. If nothing fails, S1 has no power and is VOID.

### S2: lattice rank count for Lemma B (`scripts/s2_lattice_ranks.py`)
The integer D_w(V) := dim Z(V) − dim B_w(V) on the cubic lattice ℤ³ with forward differences, where
- Z(V) = constrained fields T supported on the site set V (Maxwell: div T = 0; Calabi: T symmetric, div T = 0; Cotton: T symmetric traceless, div T = 0);
- B_w(V) = {C f : f supported in the w-thickening V_w} ∩ Z(V), with C = curl, inc, Cotton–York built from the same commuting forward differences (so div∘C = 0 exactly).
D_w = 0 says "every constrained field in V has a potential supported within w sites of V". D_w is non-increasing in w. Ranks are computed exactly mod a prime where size allows, otherwise by SVD with the singular-value gap reported.

Regions (fixed now):
- ball: |x|² ≤ 9; V_w: |x| ≤ 3 + w; w = 0, 1, 2, 3.
- cube: [0,4]³; V_w: [−w, 4+w]³; w = 0, 1, 2, 3.
- shell (π₂ but no π₁): 2 < |x| ≤ 4; V_w: 2 − w/2 < |x| ≤ 4 + w for w = 0, 1, 2 (cavity kept open).
- ring (plant, π₁ ≠ 0): 5 ≤ max(|x|,|y|) ≤ 6, z = 0; V_w: 5 − w ≤ max(|x|,|y|) ≤ 6 + w, |z| ≤ w; w = 0, 1, 2, 3 (hole kept open).
- filled ring: the ring's V_w with the hole filled in (a slab), at the same w: the charges must become locally generated.

Registered predictions at the plateau (the smallest w₀ ≤ 3 from which the ball value stops changing):

| complex | ball | cube | shell | ring (plant) | filled ring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maxwell (calibration, answer known) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Calabi (linearised Einstein, 6 Killing vectors) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 6 | 0 |
| Cotton (helicity 2, 10 conformal Killing vectors) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 0 |

Reading rules:
- If the Maxwell calibration row does not come out (0, 0, 0, 1, 0), the lattice method is unfit and S2 is VOID (no evidence either way).
- If Maxwell passes and the Cotton ball, cube or shell has D_w > 0 at every w ≤ 3, S2 FAILS: that would be a local obstruction the proof says cannot exist, and Lemma B must be re-examined before any bin other than OPEN.
- If the Cotton ring gives a plateau other than 10, or 0, the charge count in step 7 is wrong (report; it does not by itself touch the ball theorem).
- The ring is the plant that shows the count can see a duality violation. The shell tests the cderive §3 statement that π₂ (sphere flux) gives no obstruction.
- A cell that does not fit memory or time is reported NOT RUN, with the largest w reached.

**What S2 cannot see.** It is a finite-dimensional check of the topological step only. It says nothing about Lemma A (closure in the Hilbert-space topology) or about Araki's theorem. Those rest on the written proof.
