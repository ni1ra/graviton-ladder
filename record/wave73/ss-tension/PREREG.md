## 1. The two claims, exactly

### Satishchandran-Sorce (SS), arXiv:2607.20678v1

- Setting of the theorems: free massless Klein-Gordon field, Minkowski space, dimension D >= 3,
  unit causal diamond D, null Cauchy slice C- (past boundary), variable Pi = d_u(u^{(D-2)/2} phi).
  Vacuum on C-: (1.4)/(3.4), <Pi Pi> = -(1/4pi) delta_{S^{D-2}} / (u1 - u2 - i eps)^2; its modular flow
  is the diffeomorphism generated by 2 pi u(1-u) d_u (p. 4, p. 11).
- Theorem 5.1 (p. 24): for D >= 3, any faithful normal semifinite weight on the diamond algebra within
  H_vac whose modular flow on C- is an angle-preserving diffeomorphism is e^c omega_vac. Scalar only.
- p. 3: for a spacelike slice the existence question is "an open question"; the difficulty named is
  that the flow of an operator on the slice leaves the slice.
- p. 24: "massless gauge fields like the free graviton or the free Maxwell field (which is
  non-conformal in D != 4)". The parenthesis is attached to Maxwell only (page image checked; the text
  extraction drops the slash of the "not equal" sign).
- p. 26 (Section 6.2): "except in conformally invariant settings (like the Maxwell field in D = 4),
  the state omega_0 with null-local modular flow will not be the vacuum state"; whether these states
  lie in the vacuum sector needs "additional work".
- Appendix B (p. 27): "this state will not generally be obtained by a restriction of the global vacuum."
- B.1 Maxwell, any D: algebra of A[f], f divergence free (gauge invariant). Gauge A_v = 0 near C-,
  A_u = 0 on C-; free data A_A(u, x) up to A_A -> A_A + D_A lambda_0(x); rescaled u^{(D-4)/2} A_A;
  state (B.13): -(1/pi) int F1^A F2_A / (u12 - i eps)^2.
- B.2 gravitons, any D: algebra A_GR of gamma[f] = int gamma_ab f^ab with f symmetric and divergence
  free (B.16), i.e. gauge-invariant observables; restricted to a diamond assumed to be invariantly
  defined. Gaussian null gauge (B.20); free data on C- is the trace-free angular part s_AB (B.22),
  in a frame "unit normalized with respect to the round metric on the unit sphere";
  symplectic form (B.23) = (1/32 pi G) int du dOmega u^{D-6} (s1 d_u s2 - s2 d_u s1);
  S_AB = u^{(D-6)/2} s_AB (B.24); gamma[f] = -(1/16 pi G) int F^AB d_u S_AB (B.25), with
  F_ab = u^{(D-6)/2} (Ef)_ab|C- "in Gaussian null gauge"; state (B.26):
  omega_0^GR(gamma[f1] gamma[f2]) = (1/(32 pi^2 G)) int F1_AB F2^AB delta/(u1 - u2 - i0+)^2.
  Footnote 12: s_AB = O(u^2) near the tip.
- p. 32, verbatim: "It is straightforward to show that omega_0^GR defines a state on A^GR(D) which has
  geometric modular flow on C-. Since the linearized gravitational field is not conformally invariant,
  omega_0^GR is not the restriction of the vacuum state to D."
- So: algebra = gauge-invariant observables (the same content as the linearised curvature), expressed
  through the shear potential s_AB in a gauge; region = diamond, data on its past null cone; dimension
  = general D with no exception stated for gravitons; state = omega_0^GR, asserted not to be the vacuum.
  The sign of (B.26) as printed is + while (B.13) and (3.4) carry -; with + the form is not positive,
  so I read it with the sign of (B.13).

### Ours

- `wave70/modflow/REPORT.md` section 1, Theorem (a)-(d) (lines 24-38): D = 4, K(O_R) = closed real span
  of smeared linearised curvature (E, B) in the double cone; Delta^{it} e_F = e_{Lambda_{-2 pi t *}F};
  the curvature is transported along the conformal Killing flow zeta_R with weight 3. Lemma 1 (line 56):
  the curvature two-point function is invariant under all 15 conformal Killing fields (exact).
- `wave73/subspace/REPORT.md` section 1 (line 19) and section 3: on each odd sector l, the map
  (h, K) -> (psi, psi_dot), psi = x.B.x, is a symplectic bijection, local in r, carrying the graviton
  vacuum to c_l times the massless scalar vacuum; with Hislop-Longo, K_Omega = 2 pi c_l E_1[psi]
  (psi has weight 1). Assumption stated there: the ball algebra is the curvature net; odd tower.

Both sides therefore speak about gauge-invariant observables of the same diamond. SS's p. 32 sentence,
read at D = 4, says the state with geometric null flow is not the vacuum; ours says the D = 4 vacuum's
flow is geometric on the curvature.

## 2. Resolutions to decide between

(R-a) different algebras (potential versus curvature); (R-b) different object for another reason
(dimension, region, meaning of "geometric", weight, zero modes, tip or edge behaviour of the shear);
(R-c) ours is wrong; (R-d) their p. 32 sentence is wrong or too broad at D = 4.

Working hypothesis before computing (belief, to be tested, not assumed): at D = 4 the vacuum restricted
to C-, expressed in S_AB, IS (B.26), so the sentence is too broad at D = 4 in the same way their own
Maxwell remark makes D = 4 an exception; at D != 4 their sentence is right. Secondary hypothesis: the
flow of that state on the gauge-invariant algebra is geometric on the curvature (weight 1 on psi), not
the literal weight-0 flow on S, because S is tied to the curvature by Psi_0 = kappa S''/u and to
psi by a transform T with one integration along the generator.

## 6. Post-run errata (added after the runs; nothing above was edited)

- T4's expectation for D = 5, 6 was wrong. F(k, m) and R(k, m, k, m) contain only derivatives tangent to
  the cone, so the correlator between two different generators vanishes in every dimension. T4 does not
  discriminate dimensions; the script reports it as INFO. D != 4 was therefore not tested.
- T3 was widened after the first run from l = 2..4 at two point pairs to l = 2..6 at four point pairs.
  First-run log kept (`logs/cone_sector_run1.log`).
- T6 failed on its first run through the integrator (sympy returned unevaluated Meijer G functions; log
  kept as `logs/shear_curv_run1.log`), and a second attempt with `nsimplify` produced spurious
  closed forms (log overwritten). The final version evaluates each term as an exact Gamma-function
  value. The registered statement did not change.
- Plants P0 (T0 without background terms), P5 (S in place of Q) and P7 (literal weight-0 flow on S)
  were added while writing the scripts, before their first run, but are not in section 3.
- T7c is a by-hand line printed by the script, not a machine check.
