## Mismatches between printed hypotheses and our use

None that breaks a step. Four points of wording:
1. HL's algebra is generated by fields smeared over spacetime in the double cone, while modgen and assembly work with the ball's time-zero data. Equating the two one-particle subspaces is a standard time-slice step, but HL does not state it. HL's cone is the unit cone; radius R needs dilation covariance (one clause).
2. HL works out d = 3 explicitly and says other dimensions are analogous; we use d = 3 only. Fine.
3. BGL needs the vacuum to be cyclic for the local algebras. Lledó Prop. 5.8 does not list it; BGL derives it from Reeh–Schlieder (positive energy plus additivity), and for a Fock net it is standard. Say "by Reeh–Schlieder" if the bridge cites Lledó plus BGL.
4. Lledó's geometric-modular-group sentence is a remark citing BGL §2 and Hislop 1988 §4, with no proof of its own. Cite Lledó Prop. 5.8 for the hypotheses and BGL Thm 2.3(ii) for the conclusion, not Lledó's remark alone.
